# Conceptual Ad Prompt Kit — Prompts produits

Version préparée pour Boris / Parlons IA.

Ces prompts sont inspirés par la logique créative du thread de référence, mais reformulés et adaptés. L'objectif n'est pas de copier les prompts d'origine : l'objectif est de garder leur mécanique créative.

La mécanique à conserver :

```text
Produit -> vérité humaine -> métaphore physique -> composition lisible -> rendu campagne -> texte court avec personnalité.
```

Règle importante : l'image doit faire passer l'idée avant même qu'on lise le texte.

---

## Bloc commun à garder dans tous les prompts

À ajouter ou garder mentalement dans chaque génération :

```text
The campaign should not feel overly clean, sterile, or generic.
It should have a clear personality, cultural tension, and a memorable creative attitude.
The image should feel designed by a strong creative team, not by a safe corporate template.

Clean execution, but not a bland idea.

Avoid safe corporate slogans.
Avoid generic motivational lines.
Avoid over-explaining the concept.

The copy should be short, sharp, human, and slightly unexpected.
It should sharpen the visual idea, not explain it.
```

## 1. Casio — montre digitale

### Idée

La montre ne vend pas juste l'heure. Elle vend une forme de simplicité dans un monde saturé d'écrans, de notifications et de batteries faibles.

### Punchlines possibles

- `Time, without the drama.`
- `Low battery isn't my problem.`
- `Still on time.`

### Prompt

```text
Create a 3-image conceptual advertising campaign for a Casio-style digital watch.

If official brand assets are provided and authorized, use them respectfully.
If not, create a fictional minimalist digital watch brand with the same emotional territory: simple, reliable, practical, timeless.

Generate 3 separate campaign images, not a collage.
Each image must use a different creative mechanism, different composition, and different visual code.
All 3 images must feel like the same campaign world.

ACT AS:
A Cannes Lions-winning advertising creative director, minimalist product photographer, Apple-level art director, and emotionally intelligent brand strategist.

CAMPAIGN TRUTH:
A simple digital watch is not just a timepiece.
It is freedom from digital chaos.
It tells time without asking for attention, charging cables, software updates, or emotional energy.

The emotional idea:
time without the drama.

CORE CREATIVE RULE:
The watch must physically create the concept.
It should not just sit on a table.
It should interrupt, simplify, ignore, or outlast the digital chaos around it.

Use ONE dominant visual metaphor per image.
The concept must be understood within 2 seconds.

IMAGE 1 — BATTERY PANIC
Concept:
A smartphone is surrounded by low-battery warnings, charging cables, and notification clutter.
The digital watch sits calmly beside it, perfectly readable, unaffected.
The watch visually represents calm reliability.

Copy direction:
"Low battery isn't my problem."

IMAGE 2 — NOTIFICATION WEATHER
Concept:
Notifications fall like visual noise or digital rain around the scene.
The watch creates a small clean zone where only the time matters.
No fantasy glow. Keep it minimal and physically believable.

Copy direction:
"Time, without the drama."

IMAGE 3 — STILL ON TIME
Concept:
A chaotic modern desk with devices, chargers, tabs, notes, and alerts.
Everything feels busy except the watch, which becomes the simplest object in the frame.
The visual should make the watch feel almost stubbornly useful.

Copy direction:
"Still on time."

VISUAL STYLE:
Minimal product advertising.
Clean but not sterile.
Premium practical design.
Warm neutral background, black resin, LCD detail, subtle retro-modern feel.

COMPOSITION:
Strong negative space.
One hero watch.
Minimal props.
Readable silhouette.
Billboard clarity.

DO NOT:
Do not make it look like a generic smartwatch ad.
Do not overuse neon tech visuals.
Do not add random luxury props.
Do not make the watch look futuristic.
Do not clutter the composition.

FINAL QUALITY:
Photorealistic product campaign.
Minimal conceptual advertising.
Sharp copy with personality.
4:5 aspect ratio.
Campaign-ready.
```

## Note d'utilisation

Pour un carousel TikTok, le plus simple est de générer 1 ou 2 images par produit, puis de garder les meilleures.

Pour la page du site, utiliser plutôt 3 à 4 images fortes :

- une issue du thread original, avec crédit
- une image produit physique
- une image service local
- une image app / service abstrait

Si l'affiche contient beaucoup de texte, ne pas demander à l'IA de tout écrire dans l'image. Générer le visuel et les zones de texte, puis ajouter le vrai texte manuellement dans Canva, Figma ou HTML/CSS.
