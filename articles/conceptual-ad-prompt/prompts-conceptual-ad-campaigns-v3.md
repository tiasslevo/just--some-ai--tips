# Conceptual Ad Prompt Kit — Prompts produits V3

Version plus détaillée.

La V2 corrigeait la direction, mais les prompts restaient trop proches d'un brief créatif.  
Cette V3 est pensée comme un vrai fichier de génération : chaque prompt est plus dense, plus autonome, plus visuel, avec une DA claire, une logique de typo, une hiérarchie publicitaire et des contraintes anti-répétition.

Principe : chaque prompt demande 3 affiches distinctes pour un même produit.  
Tu peux aussi ne garder qu'un seul poster si tu veux générer image par image.

---

## Bloc optionnel à ajouter si tu fournis une DA en pièce jointe

À coller au début de n'importe quel prompt si tu ajoutes un logo, une capture Instagram, un packaging, un site, une ancienne affiche, un menu, une photo produit, etc.

```text
Before generating the posters, analyze all attached brand references.

Extract only the useful brand signals:
- color palette
- typography personality
- product category codes
- photo style
- logo placement logic
- spacing rhythm
- tone of voice
- packaging details
- visual motifs that feel ownable

Use those signals to make the posters feel like an upgraded version of the existing brand.
Do not copy the references literally.
Do not invent a totally unrelated brand direction if the references are usable.
If the references look weak or inconsistent, keep the strongest cues and make the system cleaner, sharper and more premium.
```

---

## Règle générale à garder

```text
Avoid lazy explanation.
Do not rely on sticky notes, random papers, receipts, whiteboards, labels, or written props to explain the idea.

The concept must be visible before reading the copy.
The text should sharpen the idea, not carry the whole concept.

Each poster must use a different visual mechanism:
- shadow metaphor
- object behavior
- product affecting the environment
- scale contrast
- material transformation
- interruption of a bad choice
- reserved space
- before/after implied in one image
- object-as-tool
- path or movement

Use a classic but light advertising hierarchy:
1. brand or product name
2. short campaign line
3. optional supporting line
4. small practical footer if useful

The typography must match the product category.
Do not use the same generic minimal sans-serif everywhere.
```

# 1. Casio-style Digital Watch

## Intention

Campagne pour une montre digitale simple, fiable, non-smartwatch.  
Le message : dans un monde qui met à jour, notifie, charge, bug et attire l'attention, une montre digitale fait juste son job. Elle donne l'heure. Sans drama.

## Prompt prêt à générer

```text
Create a 3-image conceptual advertising campaign for a simple Casio-style digital wristwatch.

Generate 3 separate vertical posters in 4:5 ratio.
Do not create a collage.
Each poster must feel like part of the same campaign, but each one must use a different composition and a different visual mechanism.

If authorized product references are provided, use them.
If not, create a fictional simple digital watch brand inspired by classic black resin digital watches, without copying protected logos or exact Casio branding.

CAMPAIGN IDEA:
The watch is not smarter than your phone.
That is the point.
It does not ask for updates, attention, charging rituals, subscriptions, notifications or permissions.
It gives time clearly, calmly, and reliably.

The visual concept must be understandable before reading the copy.
Do not use sticky notes, written reminders, paper labels, random UI labels, or text props to explain the idea.

POSTER 1 — "No update required."
Scene:
A clean desk surface in soft morning light.
The digital watch sits in the center, perfectly sharp, displaying the time clearly.
Around it, cropped at the edges, are modern devices in quiet failure states: a phone face-down beside a charging cable, a laptop corner with a dim loading glow, wireless earbuds with one case light blinking.
Do not show readable UI text on those devices.
The devices should feel like background noise, not the heroes.

Composition:
Top-down or slightly angled still-life.
The watch is the only fully readable object.
Strong negative space in the top third for the headline.
The devices form an imperfect circle around the watch, but never touch it.

Copy:
Main line: "No update required."
Small support line: "Time, without the drama."
Footer: fictional brand name, model name, website placeholder.

Typography:
Use compact mono or digital-inspired utilitarian sans.
Precise, small, technical, confident.
The type should feel like it belongs to a reliable instrument, not a luxury fashion brand.

POSTER 2 — "Still on time."
Scene:
The watch is placed near the edge of a table.
Its shadow stretches across the surface and becomes a perfectly aligned time-grid or subtle clock-like geometry.
The environment around the shadow is slightly messy: cable curve, notebook edge, keys, half-visible phone, but the shadow cuts through it with precision.
The metaphor is order inside everyday chaos.

Composition:
Low side light, strong shadow.
The watch sits off-center.
The shadow becomes the hero graphic element.
Leave clean room for the headline near the shadow, not over the product.

Copy:
Main line: "Still on time."
Small support line: "Even when everything else is doing too much."
Footer: model name and small product descriptor.

Typography:
Compact mono with tight hierarchy.
Headline medium size, not huge.
Support text tiny but readable.

POSTER 3 — "Time, without the drama."
Scene:
A simple digital watch rests on a plain surface.
Around it, charging cables and glossy devices curve around the watch like a small storm or orbit.
The watch sits in the calm center and needs nothing.
No magical effects. No neon. No sci-fi.
The cables are elegant compositional lines, not clutter.

Composition:
Centered calm object with circular movement around it.
Shallow depth of field.
Warm grey, graphite, black resin, muted LCD green accent.

Copy:
Main line: "Time, without the drama."
Footer: brand name, model, "available now" or website placeholder.

Typography:
Small, instrument-like, almost like product manual typography but refined.

VISUAL STYLE FOR ALL 3:
Photorealistic campaign poster.
Modern but not futuristic.
Retro-digital utility.
Muted graphite, black, warm grey, off-white, LCD green.
Soft realistic shadows.
No over-polished luxury look.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
Do not make it a smartwatch campaign.
Do not show giant notification text.
Do not use sticky notes or paper reminders.
Do not add fake app screens.
Do not use neon cyberpunk styling.
Do not make the typography generic luxury sans.
```

# 2. Malta-style Malt Drink

## Intention

Campagne pour une boisson maltée : dense, chaude, généreuse, avec du poids et du caractère.  
Il faut éviter la simple bouteille premium posée sur fond marron. La boisson doit visuellement avoir une présence disproportionnée.

## Prompt prêt à générer

```text
Create a 3-image conceptual advertising campaign for a Malta-style malt beverage.

Generate 3 separate vertical posters in 4:5 ratio.
Do not create a collage.
The 3 posters must belong to the same campaign but use different compositions and different visual metaphors.

If authorized brand references are provided, use them.
If not, create a fictional malt beverage brand with rich amber, brown, cream and gold visual codes.
Do not make it look like beer.

CAMPAIGN IDEA:
This is a small bottle with heavy presence.
The drink feels dense, warm, grounded, sweet, malty, almost like liquid weight.
It is not light, not watery, not forgettable.

The concept must be visual before the viewer reads the copy.
Do not use written props, paper signs, labels outside the actual product packaging, or explanatory objects.

POSTER 1 — "Open the heavyweight."
Scene:
A small dark glass malt bottle stands on a clean cream surface.
The surface subtly bends or compresses under the bottle, as if the small bottle has unexpected weight.
The bending must be realistic and elegant, not cartoonish.
Tiny crumbs or condensation can add realism, but keep the image clean.

Composition:
Low angle product hero.
Bottle slightly below center.
Big negative space above for headline.
The surface compression is visible but understated.

Copy:
Main line: "Open the heavyweight."
Support line: "Rich malt. Small bottle. Big mood."
Footer: fictional brand name, flavor, "available now", website or store placeholder.

Typography:
Bold condensed sans with warm FMCG energy.
Strong shelf presence.
Not luxury perfume typography.
Not childish.

POSTER 2 — "Small bottle. Heavy mood."
Scene:
The bottle sits in a pool of warm amber light.
Its shadow is oversized, dense and rich, almost like a heavyweight silhouette.
The object remains small, but its presence fills the frame.
The shadow should feel like visual weight, not horror.

Composition:
Bottle near lower third.
Large shadow moving upward across the background.
Headline placed in clean space where the shadow creates contrast.

Copy:
Main line: "Small bottle. Heavy mood."
Support line: "Malt that shows up."
Footer: brand name, bottle size, social handle placeholder.

Typography:
Condensed bold sans for headline.
Small footer in clean grotesk.
The type should feel like a confident beverage poster you could see in a corner shop or outdoor ad.

POSTER 3 — "Built different."
Scene:
The malt drink pours into a glass as a thick amber ribbon.
The pour forms a controlled architectural shape, almost like a column or beam, showing density and richness.
No explosive splash.
No beer foam cliché.
The glass and bottle should feel tactile, cold, real.

Composition:
Close product shot, vertical pour.
The amber stream creates the central visual line.
Use cream background and deep brown accents.

Copy:
Main line: "Built different."
Support line: "Deep malt. Full body."
Footer: product name, flavor, price placeholder or "find it near you".

Typography:
Strong condensed sans.
The headline can be bigger here, but the footer stays restrained.

VISUAL STYLE FOR ALL 3:
Photorealistic beverage campaign.
Rich amber highlights, dark glass, cream background, deep brown shadows, warm gold details.
Premium enough to look intentional, but still accessible and street-level.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
Do not make it look alcoholic.
Do not use flames, lightning, boxing gloves or obvious superhero clichés.
Do not overuse splashes.
Do not make it sterile luxury.
Do not use paper props or written explanations.
```

# 3. New Balance-style Running Shoe

## Intention

Campagne sport-lifestyle.  
Le sujet n'est pas “la performance extrême”, mais le moment où tu sors avant que l'excuse gagne. Mouvement, décision, départ.

## Prompt prêt à générer

```text
Create a 3-image conceptual advertising campaign for a New Balance-style running shoe.

Generate 3 separate vertical posters in 4:5 ratio.
Do not create a collage.
The campaign should feel coherent, but each poster must use a different visual idea.

If authorized product references are provided, use them.
If not, create a fictional running shoe brand with clean performance-lifestyle codes.
Do not copy protected logos.

CAMPAIGN IDEA:
The hardest part of a run is often the moment before leaving.
The shoe represents the small decision that happens before motivation arrives.
No toxic motivation. No gym-bro energy.
Quiet movement. Real life. Clean momentum.

The concept must not depend on written excuse notes, sticky notes, motivational posters, or text props.

POSTER 1 — "Out before the excuse."
Scene:
A pair of running shoes is already halfway outside an apartment door.
The interior behind them is warm, still, slightly comfortable.
Outside the doorway, early morning light hits the floor.
The shoes feel like they moved first, before the person had time to negotiate.
No person needed.

Composition:
Doorway as a strong vertical frame.
Shoes placed on the threshold.
Warm interior on one side, cool morning exterior on the other.
The product sits exactly between comfort and action.

Copy:
Main line: "Out before the excuse."
Support line: "The run starts at the door."
Footer: model name, drop date, website or store placeholder.

Typography:
Athletic condensed sans or sharp modern sans.
Energetic but controlled.
No brush script, no motivational quote style.

POSTER 2 — "The run already started."
Scene:
One running shoe sits on clean pavement.
Its shoelace extends forward and visually becomes a route line, like a path starting from the shoe and disappearing into the street.
The route line should be made from the lace itself, not drawn text.
The idea: movement starts before the first step.

Composition:
Shoe in lower third.
The lace/path leads the eye upward through the frame.
Use strong directional composition.
Leave top area clean for headline.

Copy:
Main line: "The run already started."
Support line: "You just have to catch up."
Footer: brand/model, colorway, website placeholder.

Typography:
Sharp sans with a slightly technical athletic feel.
Headline aligned with the direction of the route.

POSTER 3 — "Quietly faster."
Scene:
A clean running shoe on pavement creates a subtle motion echo in the ground texture.
No fake speed blur around the shoe.
Instead, the pavement dust, tiny pebbles or water trace subtly show forward motion.
The product remains crisp and grounded.

Composition:
Close hero product shot.
Low camera angle.
Negative space in the upper area.
The motion should be felt, not shouted.

Copy:
Main line: "Quietly faster."
Support line: "No noise. Just forward."
Footer: model, store, launch date placeholder.

Typography:
Condensed sport type for headline.
Small clean sans for support and footer.

VISUAL STYLE FOR ALL 3:
Photorealistic sneaker campaign.
Morning light, pavement, concrete, off-white, warm grey, navy, subtle red accent.
Lifestyle-performance, not professional athlete campaign.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
Do not show a generic runner silhouette.
Do not use written excuses.
Do not create a floating shoe fantasy.
Do not use extreme neon sports graphics.
Do not make it look like a generic stock fitness ad.
```

Campagne luxe avec humour léger.

```text
A luxury bag does not only carry objects.

The humor must be quiet and elegant.
The image should feel expensive, but not empty.
No paper-note jokes.
No cheap luxury clichés.

The bag is open on a refined surface.

Editorial still-life.

Photorealistic luxury editorial campaign.
Warm cream, deep brown, leather texture, soft shadows, polished surfaces, subtle gold if needed.
Elegant but with personality.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
Do not copy Louis Vuitton monograms unless authorized assets are provided.
Do not make fake counterfeit branding.
Do not use marble-and-gold clichés.
Do not make the campaign sterile or empty.
Do not rely on written props.
```

# Mini prompt pour transformer une idée business en prompt complet

À utiliser dans Claude, ChatGPT ou autre avant de générer l'image.  
But : remplir intelligemment le prompt sans que la personne ait à tout écrire.

```text
I want you to turn my product idea into a complete conceptual advertising poster prompt for GPT Image.

My product/business:
[describe the product, offer, audience, brand, tone, useful practical info]

Brand references provided:
[yes/no + describe attached references if any]

What I want:
- a conceptual campaign poster
```
