# Conceptual Ad Prompt Kit — Prompts produits V2

Version corrigée après feedback.

Objectif : garder l'esprit campagne conceptuelle, mais éviter les raccourcis répétitifs.

Le problème de la V1 : trop de concepts reposaient sur des notes, papiers, reçus, mots écrits dans la scène. C'est facile, mais ça devient vite répétitif.

La V2 impose :

- des métaphores plus physiques
- une composition moins paresseuse
- une typographie adaptée au produit
- une affiche plus classique dans sa hiérarchie
- un petit bloc d'informations utile quand le business en a besoin
- un texte minimal avec personnalité, sans tomber dans le slogan corporate fade

---

## Règles globales V2

À garder dans tous les prompts :

```text
Do not rely on lazy written props such as notes, receipts, sticky notes, papers, or random labels to explain the idea.
The concept must be visual first.

Use physical metaphors:
- scale
- shadow
- object behavior
- material transformation
- environment reaction
- framing
- contrast
- absence
- interruption
- replacement
- tension
- product-as-tool

The poster can remain minimal, but it should not feel empty or sterile.
Clean execution, strong personality.

The typography must match the product category.
Do not use the same generic premium sans-serif for every product.

Use a classic but light advertising hierarchy:
1. brand or product name
2. main campaign line
3. optional short supporting line
4. small footer with practical information if relevant

The footer can include:
- business name
- product name
- location
- date
- price or offer
- website
- Instagram handle
- phone number

If no real business information is provided, reserve clean space for it or use tasteful placeholders.
Do not invent too much text.

The main line should be short, witty, human, and memorable.
The supporting information should be useful, not decorative.
```

---

## Structure typographique à demander

```text
TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM:
Choose typography that matches the product:

- digital watch / tech utility:
  compact mono, digital-inspired, utilitarian sans, precise spacing

- malt drink / FMCG:
  bold condensed sans, warm weight, strong shelf presence

- running shoe / sport:
  athletic condensed sans, sharp sans, motion-friendly hierarchy

- luxury bag:
  refined serif or high-contrast fashion typography, restrained spacing

- healthy delivery:
  warm humanist sans, clean but friendly, not medical

- gym:
  bold condensed type, industrial sans, confident but not toxic

- skincare / soap:
  soft editorial serif + clean sans, calm but not bland

Use text sparingly.
The visual concept should carry the message before the viewer reads the copy.
```

## Bloc à ajouter si une DA est fournie en pièce jointe

À coller dans n'importe quel prompt si la personne fournit des visuels de marque, une capture Instagram, un menu, un packaging, un logo, un site ou une ancienne affiche.

```text
BRAND DIRECTION:
If reference images, packaging, logo, website screenshots, menu screenshots, social posts, or previous brand visuals are provided, analyze them first.

Reuse the brand's existing direction when useful:
- color palette
- font personality
- spacing rhythm
- photography style
- product codes
- logo placement logic
- tone of voice

Do not blindly copy the reference.
Upgrade it into a stronger campaign poster while keeping the brand recognizable.

If the brand direction is weak or inconsistent, preserve only the useful signals and create a cleaner, more coherent system.
```

---

## Règle anti-répétition de composition

Quand tu demandes 3 images, ajoute toujours :

```text
COMPOSITION VARIETY:
The 3 posters must not repeat the same creative trick.
Do not make every poster a centered product with a small object carrying text.

Use 3 different mechanisms selected from this list:
- product changes the environment
- product interrupts an action
- product creates a shadow metaphor
- product reveals or erases something
- product becomes a tool
- product creates a path
- product occupies an unexpected social space
- product controls scale or weight
- product organizes chaos
- product protects a calm zone

The message must be understandable from the image before reading the copy.
```

## 1. Casio / montre digitale

### Angle corrigé

Éviter le cliché notifications + papiers. La montre doit incarner la simplicité par contraste physique : elle traverse le chaos sans y participer.

### Punchlines

- `Time, without the drama.`
- `Still on time.`
- `No update required.`

### Prompt V2

```text
Create a 3-image conceptual advertising campaign for a Casio-style digital watch.

Generate 3 separate campaign posters, not a collage.
Each poster must use a different visual mechanism and composition.
All posters must feel like one coherent campaign.

If official brand assets are provided and authorized, use them.
If not, create a fictional digital watch brand with the same emotional territory: simple, reliable, practical, timeless.

CAMPAIGN TRUTH:
A simple digital watch is not trying to be your phone.
It does one thing well.
It gives time without drama.

VISUAL RULE:
Do not use sticky notes, random papers, written reminders, or notification labels as the main concept.
The idea must be visual first.

POSTER 1 — NO UPDATE REQUIRED
Visual mechanism:
A digital watch sits perfectly still while modern devices around it are visually frozen mid-update, half-lit, or stuck in loading states.
The watch remains readable and calm.
No UI clutter. No excessive text.

Composition:
Clean tabletop still-life, watch as hero.
Devices are secondary and cropped, not dominant.

Copy:
"No update required."

POSTER 2 — STILL ON TIME
Visual mechanism:
The watch casts a sharp, perfectly aligned shadow of time across a chaotic modern surface.
The world around it feels busy, but the watch makes time feel exact.

Composition:
Strong shadow play, precise geometry, minimal objects.

Copy:
"Still on time."

POSTER 3 — WITHOUT THE DRAMA
Visual mechanism:
Charging cables, screens, and digital accessories curve around the watch without touching it, like the watch is outside the whole ecosystem.
It does not fight the chaos. It simply does not need it.

Composition:
Centered or slightly off-center watch, clean negative space, subtle cable movement.

Copy:
"Time, without the drama."

TYPOGRAPHY:
Use a compact mono or digital-inspired utilitarian sans.
Precise spacing.
Small, confident copy.
Optional footer:
brand name, model name, website or store location.

VISUAL STYLE:
Minimal but not sterile.
Retro-modern utility.
Black resin, LCD texture, warm gray, graphite, soft beige, subtle green LCD accent.

DO NOT:
Do not make it look like a smartwatch campaign.
Do not overuse neon.
Do not make the copy huge.
Do not rely on text inside the scene to explain the idea.

4:5 aspect ratio.
Photorealistic campaign poster.
```

## Note pour les affiches avec plus de texte

Pour une affiche vraiment business, demander au modèle un bloc d'informations léger :

```text
Add a small practical information footer only if useful.
Keep it restrained and readable.
Do not let it dominate the poster.

Footer can include:
[business name]
[product name]
[offer or price]
[location]
[contact]
[website or Instagram]
```

Si le texte devient long, ne pas le faire générer dans l'image. Générer le visuel principal et ajouter le texte ensuite dans Canva, Figma ou HTML/CSS.
